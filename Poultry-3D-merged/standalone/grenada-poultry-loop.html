Learning pathway

Activity A2 · contract 700001756-01-04

10trainings
45demonstration sessions
10coaching visits
1international study visit
  1. Pre-tour briefing — participants agree learning objectives with the project team.
  2. Study visit to Cooperativa Juan XXIII, R.L., Santiago de Veraguas, Panama — biosecurity, animal health, processing, HACCP, governance.
  3. Post-visit sharing — participants brief the wider cooperative sector; GCLL keeps the knowledge as national hub.
  4. Into the training materials — lessons feed the manuals used at the 15 sites and across the 3 cooperatives.
  5. Regional exchange with Saint Lucia and Saint Kitts & Nevis through cooperative networks.

The contract says six participants in one place and lists eight in another — GCLL to confirm the final delegation. The Dec 2025 study-tour report belongs to a separate grant.
